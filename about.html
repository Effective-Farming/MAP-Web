About Us

Growing responsibly—delivering quality, safety, and sustainability in every shipment.

Decade of Expertise

With over a decade of expertise, we specialize in the processing of Tilapia and Pangasius fillets, operating from our state-of-the-art facility in Andhra Pradesh, India.

We work closely with local aquaculture farmers, feed suppliers, and logistics partners to ensure a fully integrated and traceable supply chain. This dedication ensures complete transparency from pond to plate.
