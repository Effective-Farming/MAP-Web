About Us

Built on a Decade of
Aquaculture Excellence

From a regional processor to a globally trusted seafood exporter — our story is one of deep roots, purposeful growth, and an unwavering commitment to quality.

Our Origins

Rooted in Krishna District's Aquaculture Heartland

"We don't just process fish — we build lasting partnerships between farmers, processors, and global buyers."

Mulpuri Aqua Processors was established in 2012 in Arugolanu, Krishna District — one of India's premier aquaculture zones. From its inception, the company was built around one principle: deliver the consistent quality that global buyers depend on.

In 2022, a new state-of-the-art facility was commissioned — 1,20,000 sq. ft. of purpose-built processing infrastructure with 2 Flat Belt Impingement Freezers (1,500 Kgs/hr each) and modernised quality systems — positioning Mulpuri Aqua as a serious supplier for premium international markets.

Mulpuri Aqua Processors · Arugolanu, Andhra Pradesh

Our Approach

What Drives Us

Three core commitments define how we operate — and why global buyers choose to build long-term supply relationships with us.

01
Sustainable Sourcing

Our farmer network operates under responsible aquaculture practices. We maintain close partnerships with certified farms and feed suppliers to guarantee traceable, sustainable supply chains that meet international standards.

02
Processing Excellence

1,20,000 sq. ft. of modern processing infrastructure, IQF and 2 Flat Belt Impingement Freezers (1,500 Kgs/hr each), and HACCP-certified quality management. Every product that leaves our facility meets the specifications our buyers set.

03
Global Partnership

We don't operate at arm's length. We learn our buyers' markets, adapt to their requirements, and invest in long-term relationships — from trial shipments to full-volume supply contracts.

Our Journey

A Decade of Purposeful Growth

From our founding in 2012 to our current position as a modern, export-ready processor — every step has been driven by a commitment to quality and sustainability.

1
2012

Founded in Arugolanu

Mulpuri Aqua Processors established in Krishna District, tapping into one of India's richest aquaculture zones. Initial focus on pangasius and tilapia processing for domestic and regional export markets.

2
2014 – 2018

Domestic Expansion

Grew to become the largest Pangasius processor in the country — building a distribution network spanning 19 states, reaching 2,000+ restaurants through 75+ distributors, and establishing Mulpuri Aqua as a household name in the Indian seafood trade.

3
2022

New Facility Built for International Expansion

A brand new, purpose-built processing facility was commissioned — 1,20,000 sq. ft. of state-of-the-art infrastructure including 2 Flat Belt Impingement Freezers (1,500 Kgs/hr each), IQF tunnel lines, 1,500 MT cold storage, and HACCP-certified quality systems — designed specifically to serve the standards of international buyers.

4
2025

Tilapia Scale-Up & BAP Certification

Full-scale tilapia fillet production commenced in early 2025. BAP 4-Star Certified, targeting US retail buyers including Sam's Club and Walmart for premium fillet supply.

5
Today

Global Buyer Partnerships

BAP 4-Star Certified and actively supplying reputed buyers and retailers from the USA. Positioned to meet the demanding quality and traceability requirements of global retail supply chains.
