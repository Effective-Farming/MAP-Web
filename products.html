Product Standards

How We Process

Every product we ship reflects the same rigorous processing standards — from raw material inspection to cold chain management.

01
Raw Material Quality

All fish sourced from approved partner farms with documented traceability. Live weight checks and pre-processing quality inspections conducted on every batch.

02
Precision Processing

HACCP-driven production lines with stringent hygiene controls, temperature monitoring, and yield-based quality checks. Every fillet graded for weight and appearance.

03
Cold Chain Integrity

IQF and Flat Belt Impingement Freezing to –20°C within minutes of processing. Products maintained at –18°C throughout storage and export — with full cold chain documentation.
