Who We Are

Specialized Seafood Processor from Andhra Pradesh

Operating from our state-of-the-art facility in Andhra Pradesh, India, we specialize in the processing of Tilapia and Pangasius fillets for global retail, foodservice, and wholesale partners.

Our advanced systems preserve the natural freshness, uniformity, and shelf life of every product, ensuring a fully integrated and traceable supply chain from pond to plate.
