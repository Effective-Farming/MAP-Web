Our Story

A Decade of Excellence in Aquaculture Processing

"Every fillet we ship represents a commitment to quality, sustainability, and the trust of our farmers and buyers."

Founded in 2012 in Arugolanu, Krishna District, Mulpuri Aqua Processors has grown from a regional processor to a globally trusted exporter of premium tilapia and pangasius products.

In 2022, a new state-of-the-art processing facility was commissioned — 1,20,000 sq. ft. with 2 Flat Belt Impingement Freezers (1,500 Kgs/hr each), IQF lines, and stringent quality systems — built specifically to serve the exacting standards of US, Middle Eastern, and Asian markets.

Our deep partnerships with local farmers and feed suppliers ensure consistent, traceable supply from hatchery to the cold chain — delivered with the reliability global buyers demand.
